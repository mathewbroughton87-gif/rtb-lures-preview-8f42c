Three things worth knowing.

One at a time

Ray makes lures one at a time, so availability changes. Confirm the lure you want before you plan a trip around it.

Postage

Postage is arranged with Ray when you speak. He will confirm the cost and how you would like to pay.

Lures can be custom painted. Describe the colour or pattern you are after and Ray will tell you what is possible.
